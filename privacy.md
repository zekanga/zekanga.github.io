# Privacy Policy — ARGUS Control Center

_Last updated: 4 November 2026_

ARGUS Control Center is a personal Google Apps Script project used only by its owner.
It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

**Data accessed.** With the owner's authorisation, the scripts read and write the owner's own
Google Sheets and call public market-data APIs (for example Hyperliquid) from Google's servers.

**Data collected.** The project does not collect, store or process personal data of any other person.
Data stays inside the owner's Google account.

**Sharing.** No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

**Retention and deletion.** The owner can delete the spreadsheets or revoke access at any time
from the Google Account permissions page.

**Contact.** argustradingintel@gmail.com
